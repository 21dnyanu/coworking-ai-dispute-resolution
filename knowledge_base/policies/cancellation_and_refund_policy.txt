DOCUMENT: Cancellation, Rescheduling, and Standard Refund Schedule
VERSION: 2.0
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2026-01-01

SECTION 1: ADVANCE CANCELLATION TIERS
1.1 Greater than 24 Hours: If the customer cancels 24 hours or more prior to the scheduled booking start time, a 100% FULL REFUND is automatically processed.
1.2 Between 6 and 24 Hours: Cancellations submitted between 6 and 24 hours before the booking start time are eligible for a 50% PARTIAL REFUND.
1.3 Under 6 Hours: Cancellations requested less than 6 hours prior to start time are non-refundable (NO REFUND), except under substantiated emergency circumstances approved by Admin.

SECTION 2: HOST INITIATED CANCELLATION
2.1 If a host cancels a reservation at any time prior to or during the scheduled reservation, the customer receives an immediate 100% FULL REFUND and a 20% platform credit voucher.
