SECTION 1: DISPUTES REGARDING PHYSICAL DAMAGE
1.1 Standard of Proof: A host claiming damage to equipment, furniture, or premises caused by a guest must provide contemporaneous evidence:
- Timestamped pre-check-in photos or checklist dated within 2 hours prior to reservation.
- Timestamped post-checkout photos taken within 1 hour after customer departure.
1.2 Conflicting Allegations: If the host alleges that the guest damaged an item (e.g. cracked chair or spilled beverage on desk) and the customer denies causing the damage:
- If pre-check-in photos do not exist to prove the item was undamaged immediately prior to customer arrival, the customer cannot be held liable.
- Such disputes must be referred to MANUAL INVESTIGATION. No automated financial penalty or refund deduction can be applied.
